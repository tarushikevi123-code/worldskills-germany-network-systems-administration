1. DEVICE ROLE AND DESIGN SUMMARY
================================================================================

Weil_A1 is the first Layer 3 access switch in the Weilburg site. It provides:

- Layer 2 access connectivity for HR, Sales, Engineering and WLAN VLANs.
- Layer 3 Switch Virtual Interfaces (SVIs) for VLANs 2, 4, 6 and 8.
- HSRP gateway redundancy for the four user VLANs.
- A Layer 2 LACP EtherChannel (Port-channel1) towards Weil_A2.
- A routed LACP EtherChannel (Port-channel2) towards Weil_C1.
- OSPF process 1 participation in Area 0.
- DHCP relay towards the centralized DHCP server.
- Port Security, PortFast and BPDU Guard on end-device access ports.
- SSH-only remote management with a source ACL.

Intended HSRP active-gateway distribution:

  Weil_A1 – preferred Active for VLAN 2 (HR) and VLAN 4 (Sales)
  Weil_A1 – Standby for VLAN 6 (Engineering) and VLAN 8 (WLAN)

PACKET TRACER TRACKING ADAPTATION
---------------------------------
The implementation tracks the two physical members of the routed uplink
EtherChannel separately:

  GigabitEthernet1/0/23
  GigabitEthernet1/0/24

This was used because the Packet Tracer version in the lab did not support the
Port-channel interface as the HSRP tracking target. The design intent is to
avoid an unnecessary gateway switchover when only one EtherChannel member fails,
while allowing the peer to become Active if both physical members fail.

The captured HSRP tracking commands do not include an explicit decrement value.
Verify the effective decrement shown by `show standby` before relying on a
priority/failover calculation. Do not assume the calculated priority without
checking the simulator's displayed tracking state.

IMPORTANT HSRP ITEMS TO REVIEW
------------------------------
1. The official task specifies priority 105 on the preferred Active switch and
   priority 100 on its Standby peer. This captured configuration uses 115 on
   Weil_A1 for VLANs 2 and 4. Preserve 115 in this record as the observed value;
   document it as a deviation from the task specification unless the final
   device output confirms it was later changed.

2. The Vlan6 and Vlan8 sections contain two additional commands each:

     standby 2 track GigabitEthernet1/0/23
     standby 2 track GigabitEthernet1/0/24

   These commands refer to HSRP group 2 under the Vlan6 and Vlan8 interfaces,
   although those interfaces use groups 6 and 8 in their corresponding VIP
   statements. They appear inconsistent with the intended group-per-VLAN
   design and may be accidental or leftover commands. They are retained here
   because they appear in the captured running configuration. Verify with
   `show standby vlan 6` and `show standby vlan 8` before correcting them.

3. The tracking target is the two physical routed EtherChannel members. The
   competition document specifies tracking the physical Port-channel interface.
   The separate-member approach is a simulator compatibility workaround, not
   an identical implementation of logical Port-channel tracking.

OSPF ITEMS TO REVIEW
--------------------
1. The statement `network 10.11.1.1 0.0.0.0 area 0` refers to the expected
   Weil_C1 peer address, not the local Port-channel2 address configured on
   Weil_A1 (10.11.1.2). An OSPF `network` statement selects local interfaces;
   this peer-address statement does not match Weil_A1's local interface and is
   likely unnecessary or erroneous. Verify against `show ip interface brief`
   and `show ip ospf interface brief`.

2. The Loopback1 statement currently matches 10.10.11.2. The official task
   specifies 10.10.111.2. Resolve the addressing discrepancy before claiming
   task compliance.

3. The captured OSPF process does not contain `passive-interface` commands for
   VLANs 2, 4, 6 and 8. The task explicitly says OSPF updates must not be sent
   to those access VLANs. Verify whether this requirement was configured by
   another mechanism or whether passive-interface commands still need to be
   added. Confirm with `show ip protocols` and `show ip ospf interface brief`.

4. The original terminal transcript displayed the OSPF section twice around a
   pager artefact. This normalized record includes the section once. Confirm
   the actual process with `show running-config | section router ospf`.
